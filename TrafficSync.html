Welcome to TrafficSync

A live simulator for a four-junction city grid. Here's how to read it.

  • Start / Pause / Reset — control the simulation clock in the top-right.
  • Traffic mode — sets how many vehicles enter the grid.
  • Signal mode — Adaptive junctions extend green for the busier direction; Fixed junctions alternate on a rigid timer.
  • Weather — Rain, Fog and Storm slow vehicles, cut visibility and raise incident risk, just like real adaptive systems that re-time signals for conditions.
  • Incident type + Trigger incident — simulate an Accident, Breakdown, Flooding, Road block or Police checkpoint on a random road.
  • Add emergency vehicle — sends a priority vehicle that forces junctions green ahead of it.
  • Green corridor — Singapore-style priority routing that forces every junction along one route green in sequence.
  • Manual override — in Junction status, force any junction's signal green for either direction, overriding the automated logic — this is how a human operator or GLIDE-style traffic control room intervenes.
  • Alert buzzer — incidents and auto-detected hazards raise a flashing alert banner with an audible buzzer; mute it from the header.
  • ERP toll — a live congestion-based toll estimate, inspired by Singapore's Electronic Road Pricing, rises automatically as the grid gets busier.
  • Fixed vs Adaptive — compares waiting time, congestion and speed between the two strategies in real time.
